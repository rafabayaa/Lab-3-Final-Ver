College of Information and Computing Sciences

The College of Information and Computing Sciences (CICS) of the University of Santo Tomas is one of the leading computing colleges in the Philippines. It focuses on education, research, and community service in computing and information technology.

Formerly the Center for Development by CHED for Computer Science, Information Systems, and Information Technology, CICS continues to develop future computing professionals guided by Dominican values and academic excellence.

Vision

A leading computing college in Asia committed to the development of globally competitive and morally upright computing professionals for the service of the Church, the nation, and the world.

Mission

CICS provides quality education and research in computing sciences, producing skilled graduates imbued with Christian values, social responsibility, and a commitment to lifelong learning and service.
